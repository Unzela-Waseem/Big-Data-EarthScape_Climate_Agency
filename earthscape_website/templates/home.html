Our Mission

Making Climate Data Actionable

EarthScape is a full-stack climate intelligence platform built as a Big Data Engineering capstone. We combine Apache Spark distributed processing, machine learning forecasting, and real-time streaming to transform 146 years of NOAA, ESRL, and HadCRUT datasets into actionable insights.

Our mission: democratize climate analytics so researchers, educators, and policy-makers can understand trends, detect anomalies, and anticipate what comes next — all from a single dashboard.
